Quisora's pages and emails can carry your own look. A server-wide **skin** changes the whole design. Each
organization adds its own logo, colour and footer on top. The emails Quisora sends are templates you can rewrite per
organization.

## What you can do here

- Change the whole design for every organization: [skins and themes](/docs/branding/skins/)
- Give an organization its own logo, colour and footer: [organization branding](/docs/branding/organization-branding/)
- Rewrite the emails Quisora sends: [email templates](/docs/branding/emails/)
